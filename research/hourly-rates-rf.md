# Hourly rates RF digital agencies & IT studios (2024–2026)

Research brief for **Moscow mid-market** digital agencies / IT studios.  
Currency: **RUB**. Period: **2024–H1 2026**.  
Updated: **2026-07-21**.

---

## 1. Scope and definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **A) Client hourly rate** | What the agency bills the client (`клиентская ставка часа`), ex-VAT unless noted |
| **B) Internal cost rate** | Fully loaded cost per **billable** hour (`себестоимость часа`): gross salary + employer taxes + overhead / billable hours |
| **Gross salary** | Monthly оклад до НДФЛ (typical hh.ru / RealHR framing for agency roles) |
| **Mid-market Moscow** | Full-service / performance / product studios; not boutique premium (РАМУ top-tier) and not low-cost regional boutiques |

**Both A and B are given as min / avg / max** per role (middle grade as primary band; junior/senior called out where material).

### 2.2 Client rate (A) construction

1. Take Workspace **service-average** ₽/h as RF mid anchor.  
2. Apply **Moscow mid-market uplift ~+15–30%** vs national Workspace mean (premium / boutique can be +40–80%).  
3. Split by grade using agency/outstaff cards (Dev-Ins, IQ Dev, freelancers on design rates).  
4. Cross-check margin: client ≈ cost × **1.35–1.8** (target contribution margin ~25–45%).

### 2.3 Internal cost (B) construction

\[
\text{Cost ₽/h} = \frac{\text{Gross salary} \times M}{H_b}
\]

| Parameter | Min | Avg (baseline) | Max |
|-----------|-----|----------------|-----|
| **Overhead multiplier \(M\)** on gross | **1.5×** | **2.0×** | **2.5×** |
| **Billable hours / month \(H_b\)** | **140** | **130** | **110** |

\(M\) typically packs: employer socials (~7.6% IT-accredited … ~30% standard), tools, office/remote OH, non-billable staff share, PTO/sick, training, tenders (РАМУ notes tender time rising to ~30% involvement).

**Utilization assumptions (relevant to \(H_b\)):**

| Role cluster | Target utilization | Billable h/mo | Notes |
|--------------|-------------------|---------------|-------|
| Delivery (dev, design, SEO/PPC ops) | 70–80% | 120–140 | ~168 paid hours calendar |
| Research / strategy / consulting | 55–70% | 100–120 | More non-billable sales & workshops |
| PM / account | 50–65% billed as own line | 90–120 | Much time sold inside other roles’ packages |
| Art director / brand strategist | 50–65% | 90–110 | Review + pitches |

E-Promo reference model: ~**941 client-paid hours/year** (~78 h/mo) at aggressive non-billable load — use as **stress-case**, not baseline. Skillbox production example: **6 productive h/day** → ~120–130 h/mo.

**Quick check (Middle SEO, gross 110 k):**  
avg cost = \(110\,000 \times 2.0 / 130 \approx 1\,690\) ₽/h → client mid ~2 400–2 800 is consistent with ~35–45% margin.

> **Disclaimer:** Figures are **market synthesis**, not a quote. РАМУ full numeric cards are member/PDF-gated; public articles confirm structure and +20% YoY (2024) but not every role cell. Where open data are thin, bands are **interpolated** from adjacent roles + salary→cost math and marked *(est.)*.

## 6. Utilization & costing rules of thumb

1. **Never divide salary by 168** for cost hour — use **110–140 billable**.  
2. **\(M = 1.5\)** = lean remote boutique; **\(M = 2.0\)** = standard mid-market; **\(M = 2.5\)** = office-heavy + sales/tender load (РАМУ-like).  
3. **Target markup:** client / cost ≈ **1.4–1.7** on delivery; **1.6–2.2** on strategy/creative.  
4. **PM/Account:** either (a) explicit line at 2.5–3k, or (b) **12–18%** uplift on production hours.  
5. **2026 outstaff softening** (− up to ~35% on mass profiles): **client** rates may lag **salary** inflation for seniors — protect margin via packaging, not race-to-bottom hourly.

### Example walk-through (PPC Middle)

| Step | Value |
|------|-------|
| Gross | 130 000 ₽ |
| \(M\) | 2.0 |
| \(H_b\) | 130 h |
| **Cost** | 2 000 ₽/h |
| Target margin 40% | Client ≥ 2 000 / 0.6 ≈ **3 333** ₽/h |
| Market client avg | **~2 800** → use package efficiency / mid util. or accept ~30% margin |

### 7.3 Microservice costing template

```
Service cost = Σ (Impro_role_rate × estimated_hours) + PM_buffer
PM_buffer    = 12% of production cost  OR  explicit IMP-PM-MD hours
Client price = Service cost × multiplier (table 7.2)
Floor check  = Client price ≥ 1.35 × Service cost
```

**Example — “PPC launch microservice” (Yandex Direct):**  
40h PPC-MD + 8h AN-MD + 6h PM-MD  
Cost = 40×2 000 + 8×3 000 + 6×2 300 = **107 800**  
Client @ ×1.55 ≈ **167 000** ₽ (ex-VAT).

**Example — “Landing FE slice”:**  
24h DSG-MD + 40h FE-MD + 12h QA-MD + 8h PM  
Cost = 24×2 000 + 40×3 500 + 12×2 700 + 8×2 300 = **234 800**  
Client @ ×1.55 ≈ **364 000** ₽.

## 8. Market context 2024–2026

| Theme | Implication for rates |
